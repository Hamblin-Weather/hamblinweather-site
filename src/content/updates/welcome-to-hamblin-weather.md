Hamblin Weather brings together forecasts, radar and official alerts for 14 West Tennessee counties, from Shelby and Fayette north to Lake and east to Henry. The county finder is the quickest way to get to your local forecast. You can save a county in your browser to make it the first place you see on your next visit.

Each forecast page shows which point the National Weather Service forecast represents and identifies the station when a current observation is available. Conditions can vary across a county, especially around thunderstorms. Open the Map Room for a wider view, or go straight to Alerts for the official wording, affected areas and timing.

This updates section is where Hamblin Weather will publish forecast discussions, explainers and station news. Forecasts and official alerts can change between posts, so check their dedicated pages for the latest information.
